Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: 10 September 2025

This policy explains how order cancellations and refunds are handled on the BiteBox Platform, operated by Kawaljit Kaur ("BiteBox", "we", "us", or "our"). Because our food is freshly prepared to order, please read this policy carefully.

1. Cancellation by the customer

  • You may request to cancel an order before the store accepts it (while it is still in "pending" status), at no charge.
  • Once the store has accepted and begun preparing your order, it can no longer be cancelled, as ingredients and preparation have already been committed.
  • To cancel, contact us immediately using the details on our Contact Us page.

2. Cancellation / rejection by the store

A store may reject or cancel an order (for example, due to item unavailability, being outside the delivery area, or operating-hour limits). If you have already paid for an order that is rejected or cancelled by the store, you are eligible for a full refund.

3. Refunds

  • Eligible refunds are made to your original payment method through our payment gateway.
  • Refunds are typically processed within 5–7 business days, though the exact timing depends on your bank or payment provider.
  • Where an order was paid via a payment gateway and is cancelled before acceptance or rejected by the store, the full amount is refunded.

4. When refunds may not apply

  • Orders already prepared or delivered.
  • Failed delivery caused by an incorrect delivery location, incomplete address/landmark, or the customer being unreachable or unavailable at the delivery point.
  • Change of mind after the store has accepted and started preparing the order.

5. Issues with your order

If you received an incorrect, incomplete, or unsatisfactory order, contact us within 24 hours of delivery with your order number and details. We will review the issue in good faith and, where appropriate, offer a replacement or refund.

6. How to request a refund

Reach out through our Contact Us page with your order number, registered mobile number, and a description of the issue. We aim to respond promptly.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The revised version will be posted here with an updated "Last updated" date.
