Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 September 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how Kawaljit Kaur ("BiteBox", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the BiteBox website and application (the "Platform"). By using the Platform, you consent to the practices described here.

1. Information we collect

  • Identity & contact: your name and mobile number, used to verify you (via OTP) and to fulfil your orders.
  • Location data: your device location and the delivery location you select on the map, used to show nearby stores and to deliver your order. Location is used only while you use the Platform.
  • Order information: the items you order, order amount, status, and any delivery direction/landmark you provide.
  • Technical data: basic device/browser information and locally stored preferences (such as your saved name and last delivery location) kept on your own device.

2. How we use your information

  • To verify your identity and secure your access.
  • To display stores that deliver to your location and to process, fulfil, and track your orders.
  • To communicate order status to you and to the fulfilling store.
  • To process payments through our payment gateway (where enabled).
  • To maintain the security and integrity of the Platform and to comply with legal obligations.

3. Local storage on your device

To improve your experience, we store certain preferences (such as your name and last-used delivery location) locally in your browser or device. This data stays on your device and is not shared with other users. You can clear it at any time by clearing your browser/app data.

4. Third-party services

We rely on trusted third parties to operate the Platform:

  • Google Firebase (authentication, database, hosting, and messaging) — see Google's Privacy Policy.
  • OpenStreetMap — for displaying maps.
  • Payment gateway — where online payments are enabled, your payment details are collected and processed directly by the payment gateway under its own privacy policy and security standards. We do not store your full card, UPI, or bank credentials.

5. Sharing of information

We share your order and delivery details only with the store fulfilling your order, and with service providers strictly as needed to operate the Platform. We do not sell your personal information. We may disclose information where required by law or to protect our rights and users.

6. Data retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to provide the service, maintain order records, and comply with legal requirements. You may request deletion of your account data as described below.

7. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your rights

  • You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data.
  • You may withdraw location permission at any time through your device/browser settings (note that stores may then not be shown).
  • To exercise these rights, contact us using the details on our Contact Us page.

9. Children's privacy

The Platform is not directed to children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The revised version will be posted here with an updated "Last updated" date.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, please see our Contact Us page.
