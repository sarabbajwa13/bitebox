Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2025

This Shipping & Delivery Policy explains how orders placed on the BiteBox Platform, operated by Kawaljit Kaur ("BiteBox", "we", "us", or "our"), are delivered. As BiteBox is a food ordering and doorstep delivery service, "shipping" here refers to the local delivery of freshly prepared food.

1. Delivery area

  • We deliver only within the store's serviceable delivery radius. Your delivery location must fall within this area for an order to be placed and accepted.
  • If your location is outside the serviceable area, the store may not be visible or the order may be declined.

2. Delivery time

  • All food is freshly prepared to order. An estimated delivery time is shown for each store and is indicative only.
  • Actual delivery time may vary based on order volume, item preparation, weather, traffic, and distance.
  • You can track your order status in real time (pending, accepted, preparing, out for delivery, delivered) from your order history.

3. Delivery charges

Any applicable delivery charge is shown in your order summary before you confirm and pay. The total amount payable, inclusive of any such charge, is displayed at checkout.

4. Delivery address & landmark

  • You are responsible for providing an accurate delivery location on the map and any helpful direction or landmark details.
  • We are not responsible for failed or delayed deliveries resulting from an incorrect or incomplete address, or if you are unreachable or unavailable at the delivery point.

5. Failed or delayed delivery

If a delivery cannot be completed due to reasons attributable to the customer (wrong address, no response, or unavailability), the order may be treated as delivered and may not be eligible for a refund. For delays or issues caused on our side, please see our Cancellation & Refund Policy.

6. Contact

For any delivery-related questions, please reach us through our Contact Us page.
